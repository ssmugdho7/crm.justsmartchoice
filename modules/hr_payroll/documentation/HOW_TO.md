# Payroll Hub How-To
1. Synchronize employees from CRM Staff.
2. Configure earnings, deductions, insurance, and tax profiles.
3. Review Integrated Payees & 1099 for sales-agent commissions and subcontractor payments.
4. Create payslip templates and assign them by department, role, or employee.
5. Generate payroll, review totals, approve, export, and issue multilingual PDFs.
6. Use Employee Cost & Project Profit for job-cost review.
7. Use Health Check before payroll processing.
