# Smart Choice Payment Schedules

1. Create an estimate or proposal with the complete project scope and items.
2. Choose the down-payment percentage and discount reason.
3. Keep **Automatically create the remaining-balance invoice as a draft** enabled.
4. When the estimate/proposal is accepted, the module creates a payable down-payment invoice and a draft balance invoice.
5. Stripe and other gateways charge the total of the down-payment invoice only.
6. Review and send the balance invoice when the work reaches the next payment milestone.
7. Use **Sales → Payment Schedules** to review deposit and balance invoices.

The module preserves native CRM items and totals. It does not change the core item tables or global DataTables.
