Appointment notifications:
The module scans the Appointly appointments table after cron runs and can also be scanned manually. It sends a Telegram notice for new appointments and for changes to appointment status, date, time, cancellation notes, or rescheduling state.

Webhook URL:
Open Telegram Chat -> Bot Profile after installation and click Install Webhook.
